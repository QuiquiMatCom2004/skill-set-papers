# Venue brief research snapshot

This note records the design inputs gathered on 2026-10-27. It is a historical
research snapshot, not a live statement of any venue's current submission rules.
For a real paper, `paper-venue` must use the selected venue's current guide and
record the source, access date, and confirmation state for each field.

## Problem investigated

The writing skills had no intake step for publication destination, language,
format, anonymity, or disclosures. Generic output could therefore appear
without an explicit author choice. A venue brief makes those external facts
available before outline, opening, and ending work.

## Sources considered

Official author portals and guides were considered as examples of where a
venue's requirements may be found: [IEEE Author Center](https://journals.ieeeauthorcenter.ieee.org/),
[Elsevier Author Hub](https://www.elsevier.com/authors),
[Springer Nature Authors](https://authors.springernature.com/),
[ACM Authors](https://authors.acm.org/),
[NeurIPS Paper Checklist](https://neurips.cc/public/guides/PaperChecklist),
and the [CHI submission guide](https://chi2026.acm.org/guide-to-a-successful-submission/).
These sources illustrate different requirements; none supplies a universal
rule for every CS venue.

## Recommendation

Use a separate intake skill to record external venue facts as C- requirements,
outside the W- communication rubric. Require an explicit author choice between
a named venue, generic work, or a request for a candidate shortlist. The
shortlist is a pending intake outcome; only a named or explicitly generic
choice produces a ready brief for downstream writing. The author chooses the
venue. Unknown requirements remain unknown, and agent-read fields require
author confirmation before they can support a compliance claim.

## Fields for a venue brief

Record venue name, type, publisher, paper type or track, paper language,
secondary abstract language if required, venue-declared readership, required
sections and order, abstract format and length, keywords or classification,
page or word limit, template, citation style, anonymity, mandatory disclosures,
figure and table rules, supplementary material rules, and any prescribed
presentation of results. Each field has a value, source, access date, and
state (`author-supplied`, `agent-read-unconfirmed`, or `UNKNOWN`).

The private book was consulted for the original communication synthesis, but
its text is neither reproduced nor used as a source of current venue rules.
